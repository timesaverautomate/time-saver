07 / KEEPING THINGS SIMPLE

Before we
begin.

Answers to help you
take the first step.

Can you work with the tools I already use?

We start by understanding your tools and how you work. We assess what can be automated with your existing resources and suggest alternatives where needed. Whether an integration is feasible depends on the access and features available in each tool.

Will the solution be tailored to my business?

Yes. We define the scope around your specific needs, the people involved and the requirements agreed. You do not need to know in advance whether you need Excel, VBA or an application.

What do you need to prepare a quote?

Describe the task, how often you do it, the tools you use and the result you need. At this stage, keep the description general and leave out passwords, sensitive data and confidential information.

How much does it cost, and how long will it take?

The price depends on the scope and is agreed before work begins. We provide a free quote within 24–48 hours. The proposal includes a 30-day bug-fix warranty. Optional monthly support is available. These terms do not affect your statutory rights. The development schedule is set out in the proposal to suit the project.

What if I do not know exactly what I need yet?

Start with the task that takes up your time or causes manual errors. Choose “I'm not sure yet” as the service type and describe what you would like to improve.

Do you only work with businesses?

Our focus is on small businesses, management teams, accounting and financial services. We can also assess enquiries from individuals where their needs fit our services.

What happens after the project is delivered?

The proposal includes a 30-day bug-fix warranty. You can choose monthly support, with the terms set out in the proposal.
