Cookies and local technologies

The website’s code does not create advertising or analytics cookies, install marketing pixels or retain quotation requests in localStorage or sessionStorage. Form entries remain in the memory of the open page until submission or closure. Browser autofill depends on your own browser settings.

Hosting and security

GitHub, Cloudflare and the email services may process technical metadata to provide the website, deliver messages and protect their services. No external browser challenge or decorative consent banner has been added.

Security counters and the email provider’s repeat-submission protection operate on the server side. They do not require the application to store a quotation in your browser.

Links to other services

Calendly is loaded in the booking window only after you select “Book a free call”. You can also open the calendar in a separate tab. The provider presents its own cookie choices and information; these controls are not hidden. Closing the window unloads the calendar but does not delete cookies or information already handled by the provider. Information entered in the quotation form is not copied into the calendar. WhatsApp links, where available, open the external service only when followed.

Changes and further information

Adding other technologies requires a fresh assessment. Where consent is required, those technologies must remain blocked until you choose, with a practical way to refuse or withdraw consent.

Read the Privacy Policy for information about quotation requests, the private internal record, email retention and your rights. For privacy enquiries, contact timesaverautomate@gmail.com.
